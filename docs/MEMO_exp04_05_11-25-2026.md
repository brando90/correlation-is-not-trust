## Three slide-ready bullets

1. **Proper scores lose to a constant forecast.** Judge LLMs A and A2 (GPT-6 Astra, Codex CLI, Vals; `P4_CHECKLIST` and `P8_STRICT_B`) and B (Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Code CLI, Stanford; `P4_CHECKLIST`) have RPS $0.395\ [0.313,\ 0.483]$, $0.390\ [0.304,\ 0.485]$, and $0.282\ [0.227,\ 0.341]$ on 75 files, against a constant forecast $0.077\ [0.055,\ 0.105]$. TVD runs from $0.859$ to $0.897$ against $0.491\ [0.446,\ 0.535]$; smoothed CE runs from $3.53$ to $3.68$ against $1.73\ [1.65,\ 1.82]$. Every judge-minus-constant interval is above 0. B, lowest RPS of the three, is 3.7 times the constant (`experiments/05_renaming_and_inserted_bug_on_veribench_judge_outputs/RESULTS.md`).

2. **The positive Spearman still fails the bug and rename checks.** B's Spearman with the expert target is $0.388\ [0.006,\ 0.653]$; A is $-0.004\ [-0.284,\ 0.281]$ and A2 is $-0.062\ [-0.369,\ 0.258]$. An inserted bug scores at least as high as the gold in $0.676\ [0.405,\ 0.914]$, $0.757\ [0.553,\ 0.938]$, and $0.568\ [0.405,\ 0.763]$ of 37 pairs (mean drop $0.297$, $0.135$, $0.096$). Pooled rename excess over repeat noise is $+0.195\ [+0.066,\ +0.343]$ for B ($n = 38$); binder renames move $0$ of $14$ and declaration renames move $0.375\ [0.208,\ 0.583]$ of $24$. A and A2 stay inside their noise (`experiments/05_renaming_and_inserted_bug_on_veribench_judge_outputs/results/summary.json`).

3. **Deployed VeriBench FSC fails equivalence; coverage scoring needs gates.** `claude-sonnet-4-6` (`claude-p-cli`, `M1_deployed_tc_v1`) has $V_{\mathrm{equiv}} = 0.750\ [0.500,\ 1.000]$ ($n = 12$); `gpt-5.4` (`openai-api`, effort low) has $0.615\ [0.385,\ 0.846]$ ($n = 13$). Real reference swaps flip $0.600\ [0.460,\ 0.760]$ (`claude-sonnet-4-6`, 50 pairs) and $0.486\ [0.257,\ 0.714]$ (`labs-leanstral-1-5-1`, 35 pairs). Standalone `claude-opus-5-5` `judge_only` scores `sorry_proofs` at $V_{\mathrm{mono}} = 1.000$ on both $n = 30$ sets; the full gate (format on TinyVeriBench, kernel plus format on VB256) sets the tested proof, shadowing, and missing-format violations to $0.000$ (`experiments/04_vb_fsc_equivalence_invariance_and_gaming_violations/RESULTS.md`).

## Limits to keep on the slide

Point-mass forecasts, with no calibration step. The constant is fit on the same 75 items. Three configurations, two judge LLMs, 24 golds, batches of 6. In Experiment 04, unchanged `sorry_proofs` and `false_axiom_proofs` scores match a statement-only rubric; the statement-level counters are shadowing and missing format. `theorem_spam` at $3.000$ to $3.250$ matches that rubric's cap of a 2-point spam penalty. Human correlation on these same FSC runs is a separate measurement (Exp 04 `RESULTS.md`, Limitations).
